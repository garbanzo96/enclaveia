02Context

Three questions an organization should be able to answer before using AI with its documents

45% of Chilean companies already use artificial intelligence, mostly for basic tasks, and 74% want clearer regulation1. Law 21.719, Chile’s new personal data protection law, is scheduled to take effect on December 1, 20262.

AWhat data leaves the organization, and where does it go?
Only what the task needs, stripped of personal data it doesn’t, sent to the Claude API. Every call is logged with its date and contents.
BWhere does each answer come from?
From your own documents. Every claim cites a document and a passage. When there is no support, the answer says so.
CWho approves what gets sent or changed?
The person you designate for each kind of action. Without their approval, no email, document or change to your systems goes out.

03How we work

One process at a time, with the rules in writing

  1. 1Assessment

    We pick one process, review its documents and map the data and people involved. Together we decide what to automate and what to leave alone.

  2. 2Rules

    We put in writing which data may leave, which actions need approval, and who approves each one.

  3. 3Testing

    We test the workflow on real cases from your organization. It goes live only once it meets the acceptance criteria we agreed on.

  4. 4Monthly operation

    We review errors, corrections, usage and cost. Each month you get a report and the full log.

A pilot goes through all four steps in six to eight weeks. For each task we use the smallest model that does it well, because it costs less and uses less energy.

05Terms

What we sign in every contract

These terms do not depend on the plan or the size of the client. They are written into the contract of every pilot and every subscription.

  1. 01Only what is needed leaves

    Before the model is called, a process on our server detects and replaces personal data the task does not need, such as national ID numbers, names, emails and phone numbers.

  2. 02Every claim has a source

    Each answer names the document and passage it relies on. When there is no support, it says so.

  3. 03A person approves

    No email, publication or change to your systems happens without approval from the designated person.

  4. 04Everything is logged

    Questions, sources, actions, approvals and rejections. The log belongs to you and you can export it at any time.

  5. 05A named provider

    We use Claude by Anthropic through its commercial API. By default, Anthropic does not use that data to train its models (Anthropic’s policy).

  6. 06If something fails, we tell you

    We report in writing what happened, who was affected and what we fixed, even if no one noticed.

  7. 07Monthly review

    We go over errors, corrections and usage with your team, and adjust the workflow.

  8. 08You can leave at any time

    Your documents, settings and logs are yours. When the contract ends, we hand them over and delete the copies we keep.

06Limits

What we don’t do

  • Make decisions about people

    We don’t automate hiring, grades, scholarships, credit or diagnoses. The system can organize records, and a person makes the decision.

  • Promise legal compliance

    We help organize processes and evidence. Legal responsibility stays with your organization and its legal counsel.

  • Handle sensitive data in pilots

    During pilots we don’t process health data or other sensitive data.

  • Reuse your data

    We don’t use your documents to serve other clients or to train models.

  • Replace your team

    We take away repetitive work. Judgment and the final say stay with people.

08Questions

Straight answers

Does our data leave Chile?

Yes. Claude runs on Anthropic’s servers outside Chile. That is why we send only what is needed, replace the personal data the task does not require beforehand, and log every call.

What if the AI gets it wrong?

It can. That is why every answer shows its source and nothing goes out without approval. Errors we find are logged, fixed and reviewed with you every month.

Do we need to change our systems?

No. Workflows connect to the tools you already use. If one of them cannot be connected securely, we tell you during the assessment.

Are you certified?

Not yet. What we do deliver is the documentation for each workflow, which says what it is for, what data it uses, how it was tested, who approves and what was logged.

Why Claude?

Because it can cite the exact passage of a document, read long case files in full and use tools under controlled permissions. And because, by default, Anthropic does not use data from its commercial API to train models.

What does it cost?

A pilot has a fixed price, agreed before it starts. After that, a monthly subscription per organization based on the number of workflows and usage, with an agreed cap. We send a written proposal after the first conversation.
